# Pick the Poop Privacy Policy

**Developer: Tsuv-Tum-Coding**

Tsuv-Tum-Coding respects the privacy of players who use **Pick the Poop**.

## Information We Collect

The current version of Pick the Poop does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information with Tsuv-Tum-Coding.

Pick the Poop does not currently use advertising, analytics, account-registration, or tracking services.

## Information Stored on Your Device

Pick the Poop stores certain game information locally on your device so that game features and preferences can persist between sessions.

This may include information such as:

- Game settings and preferences
- Scores and leaderboard information
- Game records
- Achievements and badges
- Other locally saved game progress

This information is used by the game on your device and is not transmitted to Tsuv-Tum-Coding.

Removing the application or clearing its application data may remove locally stored game information.

## Accounts and Sign-In

Pick the Poop does not currently require players to create an account or sign in.

## Advertising

The current version of Pick the Poop does not contain third-party advertising.

If advertising is introduced in a future version, this Privacy Policy and the applicable Google Play disclosures will be updated to reflect the data practices of the advertising services used.

## Analytics and Tracking

Pick the Poop does not currently use third-party analytics or behavioral tracking services.

## Children's Privacy

Pick the Poop does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

Because the current version does not provide user accounts or transmit personal information to Tsuv-Tum-Coding, Tsuv-Tum-Coding does not maintain a database of personal information about players.

## Third-Party Services

The current version of Pick the Poop does not intentionally transmit player information to third-party advertising or analytics services.

If third-party services that collect or process user data are added in a future release, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

Tsuv-Tum-Coding may update this Privacy Policy when Pick the Poop changes, including if future versions introduce features such as advertising, analytics, online services, accounts, or other functionality that affects user privacy.

Players should review the current Privacy Policy when significant new versions of the game are released.

## Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy or Pick the Poop's privacy practices may be directed to the developer contact information provided on the Pick the Poop Google Play store listing.
