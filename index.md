# Pick the Poop Privacy Policy

**Developer: Tsuv-Tum-Coding**

Tsuv-Tum-Coding respects the privacy of players who use **Pick the Poop**.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use Pick the Poop, including information stored locally by the game and information processed by third-party advertising services.

## Information Stored on Your Device

Pick the Poop stores certain game information locally on your device so that game features and preferences can persist between sessions.

This may include:

- Game settings and preferences
- Scores and leaderboard information
- Game records
- Achievements and badges
- Other locally saved game progress

This locally stored gameplay information is used by Pick the Poop on your device.

Tsuv-Tum-Coding does not operate an online account system or server that receives this locally stored game progress.

Removing Pick the Poop or clearing its application data may remove locally stored game information.

## Advertising

Pick the Poop uses **Google AdMob** to display advertisements.

Google Mobile Ads may collect and process certain information from your device for purposes including providing advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention.

According to Google's documentation, information collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK may include:

- IP address, which may be used to estimate general location
- User product interactions, such as app launches, taps, and video views
- Diagnostic information related to app and SDK performance
- Device and account identifiers, which may include the Android advertising ID, App Set ID, and other applicable device or account identifiers

Advertising data may be transmitted to and processed by Google in accordance with Google's applicable privacy policies and advertising practices.

## Advertising Choices and Consent

Pick the Poop uses Google's consent and privacy tools where applicable.

Depending on your location and applicable privacy requirements, Google may present options or consent choices concerning advertising and the processing of information for advertising purposes.

When Google indicates that privacy options are required, Pick the Poop may provide access to those privacy options from within the game.

Pick the Poop is designed to remain playable when advertising cannot be requested or an advertisement is unavailable.

## Data Security

Google states that data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS).

Locally stored Pick the Poop gameplay information remains on your device unless otherwise described in this policy.

## Accounts and Sign-In

Pick the Poop does not currently require players to create a Pick the Poop account or sign in to use the game.

## Analytics and Tracking

Tsuv-Tum-Coding does not currently operate a separate analytics or behavioral-tracking system for Pick the Poop.

However, Google Mobile Ads processes certain information for purposes that may include advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention as described above.

## Children's Privacy

Pick the Poop does not provide a user-account system through which Tsuv-Tum-Coding intentionally requests personal information such as a player's name, email address, phone number, or home address.

Advertising and related data processing provided through Google services are subject to Google's applicable policies and privacy requirements.

The target-audience classification and any applicable Google Play Families requirements are managed through the game's Google Play distribution and will be reflected in the application's advertising configuration where required.

## Third-Party Services

Pick the Poop currently uses **Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads** for advertising.

Third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

For information about Google's privacy practices, please review Google's Privacy Policy:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

For information about how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services:

https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

## Data Sharing and Sale

Tsuv-Tum-Coding does not sell locally stored Pick the Poop gameplay information.

Information processed through Google Mobile Ads may be transmitted to Google as necessary to provide advertising and related services, as described in this policy and Google's applicable documentation and privacy policies.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

Tsuv-Tum-Coding may update this Privacy Policy when Pick the Poop or its third-party services change.

This may include changes involving:

- Advertising
- Analytics
- Online services
- User accounts
- New gameplay functionality
- Privacy or consent requirements
- Third-party service providers

The current version of this Privacy Policy will remain available through this webpage.

## Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy or Pick the Poop's privacy practices may be directed to the developer contact information provided on the Pick the Poop Google Play store listing.
